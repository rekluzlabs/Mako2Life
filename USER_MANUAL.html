1. Choose Your Memory

Your journey begins on the home screen. Tap the central card to browse your device for any photo that needs a new life.

🖼️ [Visual: Tap to Select a Photo Card]

Supported Formats: JPEG, PNG, WEBP. For the best AI results, try to use photos where faces are clearly visible.

2. The Control Center

After selecting a photo, you'll land on the Restore Dashboard. This is where the magic is configured.

Auto Restore The "Golden Button." One tap runs our entire optimized AI pipeline.
Smart Toggles Selectively run Denoise, Colorize, Face Restore, or Upscale.
⚙️ [Visual: AI Processing Toggles & Restore Button]

3. Advanced Tuning

For those who want pixel-perfect control, the Advanced Settings (Pre-flight) menu offers surgical adjustments for our AI models.

Face Enhancement CodeFormer + ML Kit

Inpaint Fidelity

Controls how much the AI reimagines the face. Lower settings repair heavy damage; Higher settings preserve more of the original person's look.

Detection Sensitivity

Increase this if the AI is "missing" faces in the distance or crowded group shots.

Color Mastery DDColor

Inference Resolution

256px is the standard for fast, natural results. Switch to 512px for high-detail photos like complex street scenes or landscapes.

Vibrancy Slider

Add a pop of life to old B&W shots. Drag right for vivid colors, or left for a more muted, classic film look.

4. MAKO Edit: The Manual Touch

AI is powerful, but your artistic eye is unique. Use MAKO Edit after the restoration to fine-tune the result.

🎨 [Visual: Manual Editing Sliders & Interactive Crop Box]
Lossless CropDrag corners to perfect your composition.
Color TempWarm up skin tones or cool down a sky.
Light RepairRecover lost detail in shadows or bright spots.
💡
Pro Tip: Always use Face Restore on blurry color photos! It isn't just for black-and-white shots; it's the most powerful tool for fixing camera shake and motion blur on people.

5. Compare & Export

Before you save, use the Split-Screen Comparison tool. Drag the divider left and right to see the stunning transformation of your photo.

↔️ [Visual: Before/After Comparison Slider]

When you're ready, tap Confirm in the editor to apply your changes, then tap Save on the final screen. We recommend PNG for professional archival quality or JPEG for easy social media sharing.
